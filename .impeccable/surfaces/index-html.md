# Surface brief: index.html (TORRES OS desktop)

Scope: the whole site, one page. Mode: Experience.

Audience and job: recruiters and peers, equally. They look around the desktop, open the CV files, and connect on LinkedIn. Content comes from the CV; address, phone, birthdate and referees stay out. Static, no build, no third-party requests.

Memorable moment: the page boots, and the CV is files on a bright orange desktop.

Build path: code-led (no image generation in this session).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an operating system you use, not a page you scroll. It refuses the hero-plus-card-grid portfolio and the all-black terminal costume.

OWN-WORLD: The wallpaper is flat electric orange `#ff4f00`. Windows are warm cream with a 1px coffee-ink hairline and 12px corners, each with a title bar carrying its filename in mono. One window, the terminal, is dark coffee. Headings (name, window titles, entry titles) in Bricolage Grotesque, body in the system sans, mono only for filenames, terminal and boot log. Icons are authored line drawings in ink with cream fill. No pills, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees a desktop that has just booted, understands that the files are the CV, opens them in one click, notices the terminal runs the same content, and connects on LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 40px cream menu bar: "TORRES OS" left, LinkedIn, GitHub, Mail and a clock right. On the orange desktop: six file icons top left in a 2x3 grid (a single column would run into the name on short screens); the name "Gabriel Torres Gamez" as wallpaper lettering bottom left, up to 6rem, ink on orange; `about.md` open top right with portrait, short bio and the orange "Connect on LinkedIn" button; from 1440px wide, the dark terminal open bottom centre with `whoami` already run, in its own column so the two never overlap. Below 1440px only `about.md` opens and the terminal waits behind its icon.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Files open from their icon and windows drag by the title bar; `open work` in the terminal opens the same window. Motion grammar: one boot sequence, then short ease-out opens (180ms), faster closes, nothing else moves.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). "i love your desktop idea"; "i use this for my other pages, especially for the electric orange color"; "where it makes sense design technically, you can decide"; "if possible, keep the site static".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
